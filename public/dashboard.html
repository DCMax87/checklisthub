Privacy & data

        

          

            Checklist Hub is a private, read-only Trello Power-Up. It helps you
            review checklist work across boards; completing items stays in
            Trello.
          


          
How your data is handled

          
    
            
  • 
              After you authorize, the hub talks to
              Trello’s API only (api.trello.com)
              with your token — from your browser.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              Board, card, and checklist contents are kept
              in memory while this hub is open. They are not
              written to cookies, localStorage, or sessionStorage.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              Closing the hub clears that data. The next open needs a new Scan.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              Compact UI preferences (allowlist, saved views, density, items per
              group) may be stored in cookies on this host only.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              Your OAuth token is stored by Trello in Power-Up
              plugin data — not by a separate Checklist Hub server.
            
    • 
          


          
No third-party exfiltration

          

            This Power-Up does not send your Trello board, card,
            or checklist contents to Checklist Hub authors, analytics vendors, or
            other third-party APIs. There is no app backend that receives your
            workspace data.
          


          
Third-party resources loaded by the page

          

            The hosted page may load the following so the UI and Power-Up
            connector work. They do not receive your checklist payloads from this
            app:
          

          
    
            
  • 
              Trello Power-Up client —
              p.trellocdn.com/power-up.min.js (Atlassian/Trello),
              required to run inside Trello.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              Trello REST API —
              api.trello.com, only after you authorize, to read the
              boards and cards you can access.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              Fonts — Jost and Material Symbols Outlined from
              Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com /
              fonts.gstatic.com) for typography and icons.
            
    • 
          

          

            Your network path and employer policies still apply when the browser
            contacts those hosts. Prefer hosting this Power-Up on a trusted HTTPS
            origin your workspace controls.
          


          
Disclaimer

          

            Checklist Hub is provided as-is for internal workspace use. It does
            not modify Trello cards. Library and CDN availability, Trello API
            rate limits, and browser cookie rules are outside the control of this
            Power-Up’s static files.

A quick note on refreshing

        

          Three steps: authorize once, scan boards when you open the hub, then
          finish work on the Trello card. Closing the hub clears the list.
        

        

          

            

              sync
              Update status
            

            

              A light check on items you already have open here. Handy for
              seeing what’s been completed since you last looked.
            

          

          

            

              radar
              Scan boards
            

            

              A fuller pass across your allowlisted boards (or all boards you
              can access). Use this when you want newly assigned work to show
              up.
            

          

        

        

          Prefer an allowlist, Update for day-to-day checks, and Scan when you
          need newly assigned work on other boards.
        

        

          
          
        

      

    


    

      

      

        
Scan boards again?

        

          This runs a full Scan of your allowlisted boards (or all boards you
          can access). Use it to pick up newly assigned checklist work and
          member cards.
        

        

          Prefer Update status for day-to-day refreshes of items already in
          this list — it updates their open/complete state without scanning
          every board for brand-new assignments.
