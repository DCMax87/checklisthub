Checklist Hub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Checklist Hub is a private Trello Power-Up for your workspace. It reads board, card, checklist, and member data that you authorize so you can review assigned checklist items. It does not complete checklist items or change cards — that stays in Trello.

Data we access

  • Boards you can see
  • Cards and checklists on those boards
  • Member names/IDs needed to show assignees

How data is stored

  • Your OAuth token is stored by Trello in private Power-Up plugin data.
  • Board, card, and checklist contents stay in memory only while the hub tab or modal is open. They are not written to cookies, localStorage, or sessionStorage. Closing it clears that data; the next open needs a new Scan.
  • Compact UI preferences (board allowlist, saved views, density, and similar) may be kept in cookies only on the host that serves this Power-Up.

No third-party exfiltration

Checklist Hub does not send your Trello board, card, or checklist contents to the Power-Up authors, analytics services, or any API other than Trello’s. There is no Checklist Hub backend that receives workspace data — requests go from your browser to Trello after you authorize.

Third-party resources

The hosted pages may load these resources so the connector and UI work. They do not receive your checklist payloads from this app:

  • Trello Power-Up client script from p.trellocdn.com (required inside Trello)
  • Trello REST API at api.trello.com (after authorize)
  • Jost and Material Symbols fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com)

Disclaimer

Provided as-is for internal workspace use. CDN availability, Trello API behaviour and rate limits, and browser cookie rules are outside the control of these static files. Host the Power-Up only on a trusted HTTPS origin.

Contact

Questions should go to the team that deployed this Power-Up, or to the support email listed in Trello Power-Up admin.
