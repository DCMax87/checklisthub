Checklist Hub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Checklist Hub is a read-only Trello Power-Up you (or your organization) can host and enable. It reads board, card, checklist, and member data that you authorize so you can review assigned checklist items across boards. It does not complete checklist items or change cards — that stays in Trello.

Data controller: the person or organization that hosts this Power-Up and registers it in Trello Power-Up admin (not Atlassian/Trello itself). Contact them using the support email or URL shown in the hub footer when configured, or the support address on the Power-Up listing.

Data we access

  • Boards you can see
  • Cards and checklists on those boards
  • Member names/IDs needed to show assignees

How data is stored

  • Your OAuth token is stored by Trello in private Power-Up plugin data (typical lifetime is finite — often 30 days — unless the host configures otherwise). You can disconnect from the hub footer when available.
  • Board, card, and checklist contents stay in memory only while the hub tab or modal is open. They are not written to cookies, localStorage, or sessionStorage. Closing it clears that data; the next open needs Load boards again.
  • Compact UI preferences may be kept in first-party cookies on the host that serves this Power-Up (about 180 days): board ids you ticked, saved view definitions (filter ids, assignee ids, search text), density, theme, grouping, and similar UI flags. Cookies do not store card or checklist bodies.

No third-party exfiltration of board content

Checklist Hub does not send your Trello board, card, or checklist contents to a Checklist Hub backend, analytics services, or any API other than Trello’s. There is no application server that receives workspace data — requests go from your browser to Trello after you authorize.

Third-party resources

The hosted pages may load these resources so the connector and UI work. They do not receive your checklist payloads from this app:

  • Trello Power-Up client script from p.trellocdn.com (required inside Trello)
  • Trello REST API at api.trello.com (after authorize)
  • Jost and Material Symbols fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). Your browser may share IP and user-agent with Google when fetching fonts. Hosts who need stricter privacy can self-host those assets.

International users

The product UI is English-only. Processing happens in your browser and at Trello’s infrastructure under Atlassian’s terms. The host of this Power-Up determines where the static files are served from.

Disclaimer

Provided as-is. CDN availability, Trello API behaviour and rate limits (shared per Power-Up API key and per member token), and browser cookie rules are outside the control of these static files. Host the Power-Up only on a trusted HTTPS origin with Allowed Origins locked to that host.

Contact

Questions should go to the team that deployed this Power-Up (footer support link when configured), or to the support email listed in Trello Power-Up admin.
